# Research and decisions

- [Slowly](https://slowly.app/): correspondence has a distinct pace and collectible stamps. Keep envelopes and deliberate sending; do not impose arbitrary delivery delays on an existing private relationship.
- [Slowly delivery](https://help.slowly.app/hc/en-us/articles/360041922192-Why-can-t-I-read-the-new-letter-I-received): separate the existence of an envelope from permission to read its contents. Preserve server-gated scheduled opening.
- [Letterloop](https://www.letterloop.co/faqs): thoughtful prompts help people move beyond everyday updates. Include one optional writing prompt, not a feed of engagement mechanics.
- [Paperless Post customization](https://paperlesspost.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407067180187-Editing-Removing-or-Adding-a-Envelope-and-Liner): make paper and envelope customization discoverable near the object.
- [Bear typography](https://bear.app/faq/typography-options/): writing typography is a user preference; provide a small curated selection.
- [Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/features/): multimedia belongs alongside personal prose. Use ordered text, photo, doodle and voice blocks, with simple movement controls.
- [Awwwards interactive gallery](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/art/): visual exploration reference. Apply deliberate composition and materiality; avoid long page-load choreography in everyday app tasks.

## Asset provenance
Four original assets generated with built-in imagegen. Envelope uses user-provided pink stationery screenshots as visual references. Paper is original botanical rose-ink cotton stationery; a second portrait sheet reserves more room for long passages. An open envelope variation supports the opening transition. JPEG copies are optimized for the app; original PNGs are retained in design-assets/.

Envelope prompt: front-facing overhead blush cotton envelope, heart wax seal, pressed flowers, botanical stamp, soft light, pale blush background, no UI.
Paper prompt: full-frame pale milky blush handmade cotton sheet, fine rose-ink lilies and cosmos at corners, central writing area blank, no text or UI.

Open envelope prompt: edit the original envelope to lift the flap and reveal a blank cotton-paper letter, retaining its materials and lighting.
Portrait paper prompt: pale blush handmade cotton paper, portrait 2:3, rose botanical details at the outer margins, central 88% blank.
