# LETTERS

## Register
product

## Users
Two people in a private correspondence, confirmed by the user. They write and revisit meaningful personal letters on desktop, mobile, and an installed PWA.

## Product Purpose
A digital letterbox that makes personal correspondence feel worth keeping. Text, photographs, voice, and drawings belong inside letters.

## Brand Personality
Personal, dreamy, tactile. User-provided inspiration in inspo/ establishes blush cotton paper, floral illustration, wax hearts, and handmade envelopes.

## Anti-references
Generic chat, social feeds, SaaS dashboards, excessive glass, novelty decoration, and the previous newspaper UI.

## Design Principles
- The letter is the primary object.
- Keep the relationship private to two people.
- Make composing direct and customization optional.
- Make opening and sealing brief, meaningful rituals.
- Preserve writing through errors and interruptions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, visible focus, readable ink contrast, reduced-motion support, labeled controls, touch targets, and responsive layouts are implementation defaults.
